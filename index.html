Kubernetes Incident Response Platform

Detection, diagnosis and routing for Kubernetes failures, from alert to engineer.

  • Terraform provisions the VPC, EKS, ECR, SQS and IRSA roles, with no static AWS credentials in the cluster. Spot nodes and a nightly teardown keep development spend under $20 a month.
